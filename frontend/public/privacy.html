Privacy Policy

YouTube Description Editor · last updated 23 October 2026

YouTube Description Editor is a personal tool. Its owner runs their own copy to edit the descriptions of their own YouTube videos. Only the owner's Google account can sign in; anyone else is turned away and nothing about them is kept. The source code is public at github.com/karthikraoofficial/youtube-description-editor.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account's email address (the openid and email scopes), only to check that the person signing in is the owner.
  • Your YouTube channel and videos (the youtube.force-ssl scope): the channel's name and picture, and each public video's title, description, tags, category, languages, duration and privacy status. The app changes only video descriptions, and only when the owner reviews the change and clicks Apply.

What the app stores, and where

  • A copy of the public videos' details, the edits reviewed and applied, a backup of each video's details taken before it was changed, and a count of YouTube API usage. These are kept in the app's own database (Neon Postgres) and backup store (a private Vercel Blob store), or on the owner's computer when the app runs locally.
  • The Google sign-in credentials needed to reach YouTube, encrypted.

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. There are no analytics or tracking cookies; the only cookies keep the owner signed in.

Google API Services User Data Policy

YouTube Description Editor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Disconnect (top right in the app) withdraws the app's access with Google and deletes the stored sign-in. You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. The owner can delete the database and backup store to remove everything else.
