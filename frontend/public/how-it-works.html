How It Works

Bulk-edit your YouTube videos safely: preview, review, back up, and undo. Screenshots use sample videos.

  1. 1 Sign in with the Google account that owns your channel.

  2. 2 Sync your public videos and tick the ones to change.

  3. 3 Pick what to edit (description, title or tags) and how.

  4. 4 Review every change. Nothing is written yet.

  5. 5 Apply: each video is backed up, then updated.

  6. 6 Changed your mind? Restore a whole run, or one video.
